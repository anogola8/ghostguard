ℹ️ How Dispute Resolution Works

When a beneficiary is flagged, they can file an appeal. Program managers review the appeal and the detection flags. Clear Beneficiary activates the record and clears the flags. Confirm Fraud rejects the appeal. Both actions are logged to the HMAC audit chain with the reviewer's username and timestamp.
